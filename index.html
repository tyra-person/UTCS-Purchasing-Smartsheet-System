UTCS PURCHASING SYSTEM

Requester guide

Prepare the information and attachments needed to submit a purchase request.

Open purchase request form ↗

Before you begin

Use Smartsheet for non-IT purchases under $15,000. Submit IT purchases and purchases totaling $15,000 or more through UTCS Direct PReq ↗.

Students and postdocs need authorization from a faculty supervisor or PI. Have the authorizer’s name and email address ready.

Prepare your intake form

1. Requester and purchase details
  • Requestor Name and Requestor Email: enter your contact information.
  • Are you a student or postdoc? Select Yes or No. If Yes, enter the name and email of the professor, faculty member, or supervisor who authorized the purchase.
  • Order Summary: use “[Lab/Group Name]: [Description]”, for example “NCWIT: Balloons and Smiles Decorations.”
  • Total $ Requested, Purpose, and Dept group: complete each field.
  • Lab Group/Student Org? Select Yes or No. If Yes, supply the group or organization name.
  • Additional Email Recipient CC: optionally add one additional email recipient.
2. Entertainment and event purchases
  • For food-related items, select Yes for Entertainment Purchase; otherwise select No.
  • If Yes, enter Event Date, Event Type, Event Location, and Event Order Method. Order methods include ATG, Reimbursement (Person), PO/Contract, Procard/VP7, and IDT.
  • For blanket events, enter the first event date in Event Date and all remaining dates in Comments.
3. Account, vendor, and ordering
  • Account: enter the account to be billed. If unsure, enter the account name or “TBD.” Add any applicable budget line item in Comments.
  • Vendor name is required. Vendor contact, phone, and address are optional.
  • Are you planning to place the order yourself? Check the box if yes. If paying out of pocket, attach receipts. Watch for invoice or receipt update requests when the workflow reaches the applicable receiving or event-date step.
  • Will this be an Interdepartmental Transfer (IDT)? Indicate whether you are paying another UT department or entity, such as Parking or Document Solutions.
  • Use the optional Comments field for supporting information.
4. Supporting files and delivery
  • Attach the relevant quote, invoice, contract, or receipt. A file description is required for every attachment.
  • For multiple items, attach the Purchase Request Order Form listing all items. The intake form provides the order-form link.
  • For non-entertainment purchases, the form can show the equipment-use location and optional delivery or review fields.
  • Shipping defaults to Standard ground. You may enter Item is needed by and select Insurance needed, Request Tech Staff Review, or Request extended warranty information. Without tech staff review, items may not be supported by staff.

After you submit

  1. Accounting reviews the request, confirms the account, and handles any required supervisor approval.
  2. The request goes to Purchasing, or directly to payment and reconciliation through the ATG, reimbursement, or IDT rules. Staff confirm the correct destination for IDT requests because the configured routes overlap.
  3. Watch for email notifications when the request is submitted and pending an account number, when it is sent to Purchasing, when items are ordered or received, and when an invoice or receipt is needed.
  4. For a question or status update, reply to the automated notification email to start a thread with the appropriate party.
